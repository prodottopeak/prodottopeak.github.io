Darts Scorekeeper privacy policy

Darts Scorekeeper: 501/Cricket

Effective November 2026

Data we collect

Scorekeeper by Prodotto Peak works offline and requires no account. The app does not send your scores, player names, or usage data to us. It does not collect device identifiers or location or use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data on your device

Player names, match history, recorded darts, and preferences are kept on your device. If you enable device backups, a backup may include the app’s data. You can delete completed matches or discard an unfinished match in Settings.

Sharing and purchases

If you choose to share a result or backup, it is sent using the app or service you select. Where an optional Pro upgrade is offered, Apple processes the purchase through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Contact

If you email hello@prodottopeak.com, we use your email address and message to respond to your request.
