How we can help

From a first conversation
to a first release.

Start with the stage you need. We’ll agree the scope and deliverables before building.

  1. 01

    App strategy & idea validation

    Define who the app is for, research competing products and identify assumptions to test. Shape the core job and a practical MVP: the smallest useful first version.

  2. 02

    UX & UI design

    Map the main journeys, explore a visual direction and work through the screens. Give your app a clear purpose and a personality that fits the people using it.

  3. 03

    Native iOS app development

    Build for iPhone and iPad with Swift and SwiftUI. Test the core flows, prepare a TestFlight beta and refine the details before release.

  4. 04

    App Store preparation & launch

    Plan the name, subtitle, keywords and screenshots around your audience. Prepare the listing, localization priorities and submission, then decide what to measure after launch.

Before we start

A few useful answers.

I have an app idea. Where do I start?

Tell us who you want to help, the problem you want to solve and how people handle it today. A rough description is enough to start a conversation. We can help turn it into a clearer brief and first-version scope.

Can you help with just one part?

Yes. You might need help with research and positioning, app design, development, or App Store preparation. Tell us what already exists and where you need support.

What does it cost to build an app?

The cost depends on the features, integrations, design and testing involved. Share your budget range and timing if you know them. We’ll discuss a scope and estimate for your project before work begins.

Do you build for iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Our focus is native iOS and iPadOS apps built with Swift and SwiftUI. If your idea needs other platforms or a backend, mention that in your first email so we can discuss the fit.

Can you help people discover the app?

We can research positioning, App Store keywords and competitors, and prepare listing copy and screenshots. Launch planning includes deciding what to measure. App Store approval, rankings and downloads depend on factors beyond the studio’s control.
