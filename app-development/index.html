How we can help

Choose where you need help.

We can work with you from the start or help with one part. Before work begins, we’ll agree what’s included and what you’ll receive.

  1. 01

    Work out what to build

    Look at who would use your app, what they need and what similar apps already offer. Decide what belongs in the first version and which ideas need testing.

  2. 02

    Design how it works

    Work through the screens people will use, from opening the app to getting something done. Choose the layout, colours and interactions that suit it.

  3. 03

    Build and test the app

    Develop for iPhone and iPad using Swift and SwiftUI. Check that the features work together, then share a test version through TestFlight so you can try it before release.

  4. 04

    Get ready for the App Store

    Research how people might search for your app. Prepare the name, description, keywords and screenshots, choose which languages to support, and put the submission together.

Before we start

What you might want to know

I have an app idea. Where do I start?

Start with what you’d like the app to do and who would use it. Sketches, notes or examples of apps you like are helpful, but a short email is enough.

Can you help with just one part?

Yes. For example, you might already have designs and need someone to build them, or have a working app that needs an App Store listing. Tell us what you have so far.

What does it cost to build an app?

It depends on what the app needs to do, how much design work is involved and whether it connects to other services. Share a budget and date if you have them; we’ll discuss what’s possible and estimate the work before starting.

Do you build for iPhone and iPad?

Yes, we build native apps for both. If you also need Android, a website or an online service behind the app, mention it so we can work out whether we’re the right fit.

Can you help people discover the app?

We can help people understand what your app does through its App Store copy and screenshots, and research relevant search terms. After launch, we can review how the listing performs and what to improve.
