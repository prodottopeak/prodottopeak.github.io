A few things to know.

What can I use Keli for?

Generate random numbers, roll a die, pick names, spin a wheel, make teams, shuffle a list, flip a coin, choose chess colors and draw from a number set. Keep count handles scores and, with Premium, player levels.

Are scores and player levels different?

Yes. A score and a separate player level are saved independently. With Premium, you can label that extra value Level, Power or Health. Resetting scores keeps names and levels. These are manual counters, not automatic game-rule calculations or shared live sessions.

Is Keli free?

All nine core tools, unlimited manual single picks, two teams and basic scorekeeping are free. The free iOS app includes ads. Premium is an optional one-time US$4.99 purchase, with local App Store prices, for ad removal, player levels and other extras. There is no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No account is required. Your lists, picks and scores stay on your device. The free iOS app uses advertising services; see the privacy policy for details.
