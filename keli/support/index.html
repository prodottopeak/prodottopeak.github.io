Keli support

A little help.

Keli is coming soon for iPhone and iPad. For a question, idea or problem, email hello@prodottopeak.com.

Picking and saving

Choose a tool, add your choices or range, and pick manually. Saved picker settings let you return to the same setup later. Recent draw history is retained for 48 hours; saved setups do not share that expiry.

Scores and player levels

Basic scorekeeping is free. Premium adds a separate Level, Power or Health value for each person. Resetting scores preserves names and levels. Values are updated manually.

Premium

Premium is an optional one-time US$4.99 purchase with regional App Store prices. It removes ads and adds automatic picking, up to 100 saved pickers, player levels and other extras. There is no subscription. Use Restore Purchases in the app when restoring an existing purchase with the same Apple Account.

When contacting us

Include your device model, operating-system version and what happened. Please do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.
