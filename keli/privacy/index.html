Keli privacy policy

Keli is made by Venkat Aluru at Prodotto Peak. You can use Keli without an account.

Your lists, picks and scores

Your lists, saved pickers, suggestions, scores and recent results are stored on your device. Keli does not send this content to an advertising service or our servers. Recent history expires after 48 hours. You can export a backup, share a result, or send feedback when you choose; those actions use the apps or services you select. Removing Keli may remove locally stored data.

Advertising on iPhone and iPad

The free iPhone and iPad app may display advertising through Google AdMob. Google may process your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, app- or device-related identifiers, advertising interactions, crash logs and performance information to deliver, measure and protect its advertising services. Non-personalized advertising still involves data processing.

Keli requests non-personalized ads, disables Google's publisher first-party ID and optional location collection, and does not request permission to access the advertising identifier. We do not include your names, lists, choices, scores or results in ad requests.

Where applicable, a consent message explains your choices. You can revisit available ad privacy choices in Settings → Privacy & ads. If consent cannot be established, Keli continues to work without requesting ads. See Google's advertising privacy information and Google's privacy policy.

Premium and purchases

Apple processes the one-time Keli Premium purchase. Keli checks purchase entitlements to unlock features and remove advertising. We do not receive your payment-card details. Ads stop while a verified Premium entitlement is active. The native Mac app does not include AdMob.

Local settings

Keli stores preferences and local ad-frequency counters on your device. These counters do not contain your picker content and are not sent to our servers. Keli has no separate analytics SDK or user account service.

Data retention and your choices

You can clear recent history, remove saved pickers and suggestions, and manage local settings in Keli. Data included in device backups is managed through your device and backup provider. Google controls retention of data processed by its advertising services, as described in its privacy policy. Contact us if you have a question or a request about your privacy.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when Keli’s data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For questions about Keli or its privacy practices, contact hello@prodottopeak.com.
